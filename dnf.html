Cycle tracking

Avg cycle length: days
Log the first day of your period each cycle (format DDMMYYYY, e.g. 01082026 for 01-08-2026, or leave blank for today). Your average cycle length is calculated automatically from these dates, and every session's cycle phase is then estimated from that automatically.
In the Health app: Browse → Cycle Tracking → Period → the share icon → Export PDF. Upload that file here and we'll pull out the period start dates.
