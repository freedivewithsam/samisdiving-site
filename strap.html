Strap costing & pricing

Log what you pay for straps, stoppers and end locks, and how long each item takes you to make. Each material gets an average cost per unit, and the mask strap and fins strap costs and prices are worked out from that. Everything is in IDR and saves in this browser. Every number box works like a calculator: type something like 50*3000 or 120000+30000 and the answer shows underneath.

Step 1

Materials bought

Add every purchase: how many you got and the total you paid. The average cost per unit is the total spent divided by the total quantity, so bigger orders count for more.

Step 2

Cost & price per item

Say how much of each material goes into one item, add any extra costs (your time, packaging), then pick the profit margin you want.

Backup

Your numbers are saved in this browser only. Download a backup now and then, especially before clearing browser data or switching device.
